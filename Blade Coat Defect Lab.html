Model
What is measured and what is assumed
  • Yours: speed, machine height, fibre thickness, viscosity 10.5 Pa·s, design wet film 1.45 mm (the pressure default is back-calculated so the model returns 1.45 mm; it is not a measurement).
  • Assumed until you measure them: surface tension (0.07 N/m, water-like), contact angle on the blade, yield stress, shear-thinning index, bead pressure, land length, notch face length, density 1020 kg/m³.
  • Model limits: the film thickness uses lubrication theory with an effective viscosity. The contact-line height is the static gravity-plus-capillary meniscus (Young–Laplace), so dynamic wetting is not included. The web-edge and levelling results are order-of-magnitude indicators. Use them to see which lever matters, not to predict exact millimetres.
  • Conservation: the animation advances the pool with a volume balance. If the pool cannot supply the requested outlet flow, the outlet is supply-limited rather than allowing a negative liquid volume. Numerical failures stop the evolving surface and are reported; they are never displayed as a valid physical prediction.
